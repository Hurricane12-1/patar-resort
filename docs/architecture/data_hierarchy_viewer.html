LEVEL 4 Administrator

Executive Management & Technical Governance

  • Full read/write access across all system tables and operations
  • Staff account creation and role assignment (staff, receptionist, accounting)
  • One-click database backups and schema migration management
  • Global CSV financial reports and executive revenue analytics

LEVEL 3A Accounting

Financial Ledger & Billing Operations

  • Read & adjust booking financial totals and nightly rates
  • Weekly and monthly revenue time-series aggregation
  • Audit guest in-room dining and service order charges
  • Export accounting journals and room occupancy financial records

LEVEL 3B Receptionist

Front Desk Operations & Guest Processing

  • Live guest check-in and check-out status transitions
  • Walk-in reservation creation and room assignment
  • Real-time room availability calendar queries
  • Outbound SMS notification dispatch and digital pass distribution

LEVEL 2 Department Staff

Housekeeping, Maintenance, Kitchen & Tours

  • View and execute assigned department operational tasks
  • Update task status (Pending -> In Progress -> Completed)
  • Track resort inventory stock consumption and trigger reorder alerts
  • Fulfill guest in-room dining orders and excursion requests

Physical Storage & Lifecycle Tiering

Tier Medium Latency Mutability Entities / Content
Tier 1: Hot Realtime In-Memory, WebSockets, LocalStorage < 15ms Ephemeral / Active Socket.io events, typing indicators, jd_session tokens
Tier 2: Operational OLTP PostgreSQL 15+ (Supabase) < 50ms Transactional Read/Write profiles, reservations, department_tasks, orders
Tier 3: Audit & Historical Indexed Database Tables < 100ms Append-Only / Immutable notifications (SMS logs), reviews, completed stay receipts
Tier 4: Cold Disaster Recovery File System & Git Releases Offline Read-Only Versioned Snapshots backups/database/*.sql, system configs, git tag v1.0.0-stable
